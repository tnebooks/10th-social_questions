## Pulicat Lake is located between the States of __________.

---

Pulicat Lake is the second largest brackish-water lagoon in India, situated on the Coromandel Coast straddling the border between Tamil Nadu (Tiruvallur district) and Andhra Pradesh (Tirupati district).
