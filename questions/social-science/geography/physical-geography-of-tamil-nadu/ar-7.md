  -  Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) explains (A).

Assertion (A): Tamil Nadu does not receive much rainfall from southwest monsoon.

Reason (R): It is situated in the rain shadow area of the Western Ghats.

---

The Western Ghats block much of the moisture-bearing southwest monsoon from reaching Tamil Nadu, placing much of the state in their rain shadow; therefore both the assertion and reason are true, and the reason explains the assertion.
