## Who granted the English “Golden Fireman” in 1632 ?

---

In 1632, the Sultan of Golconda granted the English East India Company the 'Golden Farman' (Fireman), allowing them duty-free trade within the ports of the Golconda kingdom for an annual payment of 500 pagodas.
