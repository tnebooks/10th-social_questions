## Arrange the following in the correct chronological order and choose the correct answer from the code given below:

(i) Panchasheel

(ii) China’s Nuclear test

(iii) Twenty year Treaty

(iv) First Nuclear test of India

---

Chronology: (i) Panchasheel Treaty (1954), (ii) China's Nuclear Test (1964), (iii) Indo-Soviet Twenty-Year Treaty of Peace and Friendship (1971), and (iv) First Nuclear Test of India (Pokhran-I in 1974). Hence, (i), (ii), (iii), (iv) is the correct sequence.
