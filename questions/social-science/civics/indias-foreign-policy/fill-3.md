## _____________ was India’s policy in the face of the bipolar order of the cold war.

```markdown
Amidst the bipolar division of the world during the Cold War between the US and Soviet blocs, India adopted the policy of Non-Alignment (NAM) to maintain its strategic autonomy.
```
