## ____________ is known as the Land of thunderbolt.

```markdown
Bhutan is traditionally referred to as 'Druk Yul' or the 'Land of the Thunder Dragon' (Land of Thunderbolt) due to the violent storms that sweep in from the Himalayas.
```
