## A strip of land ____________, belongs to India on West Bengal and Bangladesh border.

```markdown
The Teen Bigha Corridor is a strip of land belonging to India on the West Bengal-Bangladesh border that was leased to Bangladesh to provide access to its Dahagram-Angarpota enclave.
```
