## Which of the following States does not possess a bicameral Legislature ?

---

Tamil Nadu has a unicameral legislature; the Tamil Nadu Legislative Council was abolished by the M.G. Ramachandran government on 1 November 1986. Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and UP retain bicameral legislatures.
