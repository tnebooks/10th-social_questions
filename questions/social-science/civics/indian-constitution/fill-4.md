## ________ writs are mentioned in Article 32.

```markdown
Article 32 provides the right to constitutional remedies and empowers the Supreme Court to issue five types of writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, and Quo-Warranto.
```
