## ________ is considered the father of Tamil linguistic purism.

```markdown
Maraimalai Adigal (Swaminathan) is regarded as the father of Tamil linguistic purism (Thani Thamizh Iyakkam), advocating for the use of pure Tamil words devoid of Sanskrit influence.
```
