## The name Suriyanarayana Sastri changed in Tamil as ________.

```markdown
V.G. Suryanarayana Sastri changed his Sanskrit name into pure Tamil as 'Parithimaar Kalaignar' (Sun among scholars), pioneering the trend of Tamil identity and nomenclature.
```
