## What was the context in which the Chotanagpur Tenancy Act was passed ?

---

Following the fierce Munda Rebellion (Ulgulan) led by Birsa Munda against feudal landlords and British moneylenders in the 1890s, the colonial government passed the Chotanagpur Tenancy Act in 1908 to protect tribal land rights.
