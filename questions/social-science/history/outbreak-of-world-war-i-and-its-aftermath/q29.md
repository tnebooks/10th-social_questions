## Which country emerged as the strongest in East Asia towards the close of nineteenth century ?

---

Following the Meiji Restoration (1868), Japan underwent rapid modernization and industrialization, defeating China in the Sino-Japanese War (1894–95) to emerge as the dominant power in East Asia.
