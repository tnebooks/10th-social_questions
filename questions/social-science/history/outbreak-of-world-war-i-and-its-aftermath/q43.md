## Who said “Imperialism is the highest stage of Capitalism” ?

---

Vladimir Lenin wrote the famous treatise *Imperialism, the Highest Stage of Capitalism* (1916), arguing that imperialist expansion was the inevitable outcome of monopolistic financial capitalism.
