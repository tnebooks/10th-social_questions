## After the death of Dr. Sun Yat Sen, the leader of the Kuomintang party was ________.

```markdown
Following the death of Dr. Sun Yat-Sen in 1925, Chiang Kai-shek assumed leadership of the Nationalist Kuomintang (KMT) party.
```
