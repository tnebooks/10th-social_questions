## ________ hoisted the national flag atop Fort St. George on 26 January 1932.

```markdown
During the Civil Disobedience Movement, freedom fighter Bashyam (known as Arya) bravely hoisted the tricolour national flag atop Fort St. George in Madras on January 26, 1932.
```
