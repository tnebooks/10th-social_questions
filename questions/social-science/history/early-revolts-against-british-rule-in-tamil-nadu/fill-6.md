## ________ was declared the new Sultan by the rebels in Vellore Fort.

```markdown
During the outbreak of the Vellore Revolt in 1806, the Indian sepoys hoisted the tiger-striped flag of Mysore and declared Fateh Hyder as their new ruler.
```
