## Who was the first Palayakkarars to resist the East India Company’s Policy of territorial aggrandizement ?

---

Puli Thevar of Nerkattumseval was the pioneer in Tamil Nadu who began the armed resistance against the British East India Company's territorial expansion in 1755.
