## The Civil Disobedience Movement in North West Frontier Province was led by _______.

```markdown
Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (known as the Frontier Gandhi) led the Civil Disobedience Movement in the North-West Frontier Province through the Khudai Khidmatgar.
```
