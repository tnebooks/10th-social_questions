## Ramsay Macdonald announced _______ which provided separate electorates to the minorities and the depressed classes.

```markdown
British Prime Minister Ramsay MacDonald announced the Communal Award in 1932, providing separate electorates for Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, and the depressed classes.
```
